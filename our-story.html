Our Story

Hello from River and Calvin!

In mid-September 2026 we spent our honeymoon on safari in Tanzania, from the great river crossing in the northern Serengeti to the floor of Ngorongoro Crater. We loved every minute of it.

Every photo on this site was taken by the two of us: happy amateurs with a second-hand long lens (400mm) and a lot of patience. We made these little games so the memories have a home, and so you can share a bit of the wonder we felt.
