What is this gazelle grooming itself with?

    Grooming in the crater

    On the grassy floor of Ngorongoro Crater, Thomson’s gazelles groom on a regular rhythm, not only when they itch, combing their coats with their lower front teeth to pick off ticks before the ticks can start to feed.

    Fun facts

    • Thomson’s gazelles, often called “Tommies”, have a bold black band along each side.
    • Both males and females have ringed horns. A male’s are 25–43 cm long; a female’s only 7–15 cm.
    • When a predator is near, Tommies often “stot”: they bounce high on stiff legs, which may startle the hunter and show off their strength.
