Blowing in the Wind

Look closely at this young giraffe’s mouth. Something is blowing in the wind.

Find the drool and trace it

Start at the mouth and follow it all the way down. Zoom in if you need to. You need 70% accuracy to win.

Tip: when you are zoomed in, switch to Move to drag the photo around. On a phone, two fingers zoom and move too.

Why was drool blowing in the wind?

Because it had just had milk from mum. So cute!

Fun facts

  • Giraffes give birth standing up, so a calf starts life with a drop of about 1.5 metres. Within an hour it is standing.
  • Calves drink their mother’s milk for about a year, and start nibbling leaves after a few months.
  • While the mothers feed, calves often stay together in a group, called a crèche, with an adult keeping watch.
