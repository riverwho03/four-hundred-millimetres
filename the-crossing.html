Fun facts

  • In the Serengeti, a hurt animal rarely survives for long. Lions, hyenas and crocodiles are quick to pick out the weak and the slow.
  • Each year well over a million wildebeest, with hundreds of thousands of zebras and gazelles, follow the rains on a great loop through the Serengeti and Kenya’s Maasai Mara.
  • Crocodiles hunt by ambush: they wait in the water, grab an animal and drag it under.
