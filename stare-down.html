The Stare-Down

A spotted hyena stands in muddy water in Ngorongoro. Something above it has its full attention. Zoom in to take a closer look.

Why is the hyena staring at the gull?

    Fun facts

    • Hyenas stash food in water. In Tanzania’s Ngorongoro Crater, researchers recorded hyenas leaving wildebeest carcasses in a lake, which may hide the meat from rivals.
    • The famous hyena “laugh” is not about humour. It usually signals excitement or nervousness.
    • Grey-headed gulls live along lakes and rivers across much of Africa, and they are quick to scavenge.
