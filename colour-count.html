    Fun facts

    • It hunts from a high perch like this one, then swoops down on insects, scorpions, centipedes, snails and even small birds.
    • In courtship it dives from high up and rolls from side to side as it levels out. That rolling flight gave rollers their name.
    • For such a beautiful bird, its call is surprisingly rough: a harsh, sawing “rak rak rak”. You can hear it in Frame 07.
