The Builder

This photo was taken in Tarangire. High in an acacia sits a dome of sticks too big to wrap your arms around, with three rooms inside! 3-bedroom house only! A hamerkop built it. Which bird is the hamerkop?

Which of these birds is the hamerkop?

    Hint: “hamerkop” is Afrikaans for “hammerhead”.

    Fun facts

    • A hamerkop nest can be more than 1.5 m across, and strong enough to hold a person’s weight.
    • One nest can take a pair 10 to 14 weeks to build, using around 8,000 sticks and bunches of grass.
    • Hamerkops are compulsive builders: a pair may make three to five nests a year, whether they breed or not.
