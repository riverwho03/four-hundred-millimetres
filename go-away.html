    The famous “go-away”

    The best-known version of the call belongs to its southern cousin, the grey go-away-bird. Its loud, nasal call is often written as “kweh” or “go-way”, and that is where go-away-birds get their name. This video from Wild Ambience on YouTube starts at the call.

    Fun facts

    • Go-away-birds belong to the turaco family, a group of birds found only in Africa.
    • The bare-faced go-away-bird’s calls include a loud, repeated “kow-kow”.
    • The hadada ibis and the go-away-birds are all named after the sounds they make.
